What's new

The most recent changes will be listed first. Not every small fix makes the list — just the bits actually worth knowing about.

6 October 2026
  • The daily screen now has three cards: the greeting with today's date and time, then a new sentence (or a short question and answer) made from words you've been taught, then one Japanese saying. The sentence uses the grammar from the course so far, with spaces between every word and particle so it lines up with the romaji underneath. Tap a line to hear it — there's a different one each time.
  • The daily sayings screen now opens with a greeting, today's date and the time in Japanese — おはようございます / こんにちは / こんばんは, the date read out (e.g. じゅうがつ むいか かようび) and the time (e.g. ごぜん くじ よんじゅうきゅうふん), each with romaji and English. Tap a line to hear it.
  • Works offline automatically now — the "Work offline" switch has gone, as it's always on. You still get updates straight away whenever you have a signal.
  • New at the bottom of Settings: a QR code so friends can scan to open the app, and a link to Kanji strokes for practising writing hiragana and katakana.
4 October 2026
  • Fixed a display bug: a few spots (the verb conjugation reference's pill buttons and headings) were showing small kana (っ ゃ ゅ ょ etc.) at full size instead of the smaller size used everywhere else — so べんきょうします could read like "bekiyoushimasu" at a glance. Fixed, and double-checked that every other place Japanese text is shown wraps small kana the same way.
2 October 2026
  • Level 3, Lesson 1 is in, under the L3 pill: Food and drink, Verbs, Hobbies & activities, Frequency & linking words, and General vocabulary. Words you already knew from Level 1/2 are just tagged into Level 3 too, so your scores carry over.
  • Level 3 has its own Grammar notes card at the top of its list: frequency words (いつも … ぜんぜん, and why あまり/ぜんぜん need a negative verb), a sentence builder with a frequency slot, を, と vs や, それから vs そして, なん vs なに, noun + します, and the お/ご polite prefix.
  • Hold any word card for a moment to see its kana, romaji and English in a bubble — and hear it said.
  • New speaker button at the top, next to ⚙: turns all the app's sound on or off, so it won't cut into your music. If you hear nothing on an iPhone or iPad, check silent mode (the switch on the side, or the bell in Control Centre).
  • Loanwords and foreign place names (countries, cities, ペン, ネクタイ…) are now in katakana, the way you'll actually see them in Japan. The hiragana from the early notes still shows after you answer. Scores for those words start again from scratch.
  • New "Time words" collection, and Core grammar words / Everyday items now include the Level 3 words too.
  • Going to Japan? Turn on Untaught vocabulary in Settings and start with Tourist essentials: about 50 ready-made phrases and 135 useful words, with switches to show romaji instead of kana and to hear each card when you tap it.
  • The Genki textbook lists aren't part of the shared app any more — they were my own extra study and are now kept privately.
12 September 2026
  • Level 3 starts here — this is a new, separate version from the one below. First addition: a "Genki" pill alongside Level 1/2 (now shortened to L1/L2 to make room), working through the Genki textbook to fill gaps and get ahead. Starts with Lesson 1-1; more lessons will be added as I go. Off by default — turn it on in Settings if you want it too. Anything already taught in the course shows up in both places, nothing is duplicated.
4 September 2026
  • That's it for this version — thanks to everyone who's been using it, I hope it's actually helped. It's not going anywhere, so keep using it as much as you like, I just won't be adding to it unless something's genuinely wrong. Level 3 gets its own version, starting towards the end of the month.
1 September 2026
  • Drilling now leans toward whichever words you're weaker on (or haven't seen yet), instead of every word having exactly the same odds of coming up — so the random tests should start feeling more useful over time rather than just repeating whatever you already know.
  • New setting: confidence colours can optionally fade back toward neutral the longer a word goes unpractised, so it's a nudge that a word might be worth revisiting rather than a permanent grade. Off by default — turn it on in Settings if you want it.
  • Weather now includes the four seasons (spring/summer/autumn/winter), and the Kanji section picked up the basic colour and season characters it was missing.
31 August 2026
  • Trip vocabulary and Andrew's old "Materials" content merged into one "Untaught" tier, with its own pill and toggle — same content as before, just one switch instead of two.
  • Food & drink split into five smaller cards (Food, Drink, Dining phrases, Food descriptions, Preparation & cooking) instead of one big 218-word pile, so multiple-choice options make more sense.
  • Fixed a couple of mobile layout niggles — the Untaught pill wrapping onto its own line, and cramped spacing on wrapped grammar labels.
